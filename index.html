About Shivansh Traders

Building Trust, One Foundation At A Time

Located in the heart of Deoghar (Rampur, near Pani Tanki), Shivansh Traders has established itself as the most reliable, comprehensive, and quality-driven one-stop destination for structural building materials, hardware, plumbing, and electrical essentials.

Our Mission

To empower homeowners, builders, and skilled karigars with uncompromised material quality, transparent wholesale pricing, and doorstep site deliveries across Deoghar without delays.

“Har Karigar Ka Sathi”
